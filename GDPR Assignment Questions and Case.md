# GDPR Mobile Application Project – Assignment

## Assignment Overview

In this assignment, you will be working on a project involving the development of a new mobile application by a tech team within your organization. The mobile application aims to facilitate socialization among your organization's employees.

The registration process for the application requires users to provide their **full name, date of birth, and email address**.

Your task is to understand the project and its implications, and then address a set of questions related to **data protection and privacy**.

> **Remember:** Carefully consider the context of the new mobile application project and apply your understanding of data protection principles, legal requirements, and best practices to address the questions effectively.

**Good luck with your assignment!**

---

## Questions for This Assignment

### 1. Personal Data Elements

**Identify the key personal data elements involved in the registration process for the mobile application. Based on the GDPR, explain why these data elements are considered personal data and require protection.**

---

### 2. Legal Basis for Processing

**Assess the legal basis for processing personal data in this mobile application project. Determine the most appropriate legal basis for collecting and processing users' personal data and provide a justification for your choice.**

---

### 3. Privacy and Data Protection Risks

**Analyze the potential risks to individuals' privacy and data protection arising from the collection and processing of personal data in the mobile application. Identify at least three risks and explain how they can be mitigated under the GDPR.**

---

### 4. Data Subjects' Rights

**Evaluate the data subjects' rights that may be relevant to this mobile application project. Identify specific rights that users may exercise regarding their personal data and explain how the organization can ensure compliance with these rights.**

---

### 5. Overall Project Risk

**Evaluate the risk of the project and provide an explanation as to why you chose that risk level.**

**Risk Levels:**
- Low
- Medium
- High
- Very High
