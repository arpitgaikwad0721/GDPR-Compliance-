# GDPR & Data Privacy Fundamentals

This repository documents my learning and practical application of **Data Privacy and GDPR fundamentals**, including a case study based on the development of a mobile application for employee socialization.

The work combines concepts learned through the **Data Privacy Complete GDPR Fundamentals** course by **Mustafa Ali** on Udemy with a practical GDPR case study and personal analysis.

---

## 📚 Course

**Course:** Data Privacy Complete GDPR Fundamentals  
**Platform:** Udemy  
**Instructor:** Mustafa Ali

The course provided an understanding of the fundamental concepts involved in **data privacy, GDPR compliance, personal data protection, data processing, and organisational responsibilities**.

### Key Learnings

#### 🔹 GDPR Fundamentals

- Understanding the purpose and importance of the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**
- Understanding the scope and applicability of GDPR
- Understanding what constitutes **personal data**
- Understanding the role of data protection in modern organisations
- Understanding the importance of privacy and responsible data processing

#### 🔹 GDPR Principles

Learned about the core principles that guide the processing and protection of personal data, including:

- **Lawfulness, fairness and transparency**
- **Purpose limitation**
- **Data minimisation**
- **Accuracy**
- **Storage limitation**
- **Integrity and confidentiality**
- **Accountability**

#### 🔹 Legal Bases for Processing

Understanding the different lawful bases under GDPR for processing personal data, including:

- **Consent**
- **Contractual necessity**
- **Legal obligation**
- **Vital interests**
- **Public task**
- **Legitimate interests**

A key learning was that organisations should identify and document an appropriate legal basis before processing personal data.

#### 🔹 Data Controllers and Data Processors

Understanding the difference between:

- **Data Controller** – determines the purposes and means of processing personal data.
- **Data Processor** – processes personal data on behalf of the controller.

The importance of defining responsibilities and maintaining appropriate agreements between involved parties was also studied.

#### 🔹 Data Subject Rights

Understanding the rights available to individuals under GDPR, including:

- **Right to be informed**
- **Right of access**
- **Right to rectification**
- **Right to erasure**
- **Right to restriction of processing**
- **Right to data portability**
- **Right to object**
- **Rights related to automated decision-making and profiling**

#### 🔹 Privacy by Design and by Default

Understanding the importance of incorporating data protection considerations from the **design and development stage** rather than treating privacy as something to be added later.

This includes considerations such as:

- Data minimisation
- Access controls
- Encryption
- Appropriate security measures
- User control over personal data
- Privacy considerations throughout the application lifecycle

#### 🔹 DPIA and Privacy Risk Assessment

Learned about **Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs)** and their role in identifying, assessing, and mitigating privacy risks associated with data processing activities.

#### 🔹 Data Protection and Security

Understanding the importance of appropriate **technical and organisational measures** for protecting personal data, including:

- Encryption
- Identity and access management
- Access control
- Firewalls
- Security monitoring
- Incident response
- Data breach management

#### 🔹 Documentation, Auditing and Compliance

Learned about the importance of maintaining proper documentation and continuously monitoring GDPR compliance through:

- Privacy policies
- Data protection agreements
- Data processing documentation
- DPIAs
- Data breach documentation
- Internal audits
- Compliance reviews
- Organisational privacy practices

# 📝 Practical Case Study

## Employee Socialization Mobile Application

The practical assignment considers a scenario where a technology team within an organisation is developing a **mobile application intended to facilitate socialization among employees**.

During registration, users are required to provide:

- **Full Name**
- **Date of Birth**
- **Email Address**

The case study evaluates the privacy and data protection implications of collecting and processing this information under GDPR.

---

## 🔍 Case Study Analysis

The case study covers the following areas:

### 1. Personal Data Identification

Identification of the personal data elements collected during registration and understanding why they qualify as **personal data** under GDPR.

### 2. Legal Basis for Processing

Assessment of the appropriate legal basis for processing the collected personal data, with a focus on **legitimate interests** and the importance of transparency and appropriate consent mechanisms where applicable.

### 3. Privacy and Data Protection Risks

Identification of potential risks associated with the collection and processing of employee data, including:

- Identification of individuals
- Unlawful use of collected information
- Data breaches
- Cyber attacks
- Insider threats
- Unauthorised use or disclosure of personal data

The analysis also considers measures such as **privacy by design and by default, DPIAs, access controls, encryption, and other technical and organisational measures**.

### 4. Data Subject Rights

Evaluation of the rights that employees may exercise regarding their personal data, including:

- Right to be informed
- Right to restriction of processing
- Right to erasure
- Right to object
- Right to access
- Right to rectification
- Right to data portability
- Rights related to automated decision-making

### 5. Overall Project Risk

An overall risk assessment of the project was performed based on:

- The nature of the personal data collected
- The number of individuals involved
- Potential privacy impacts
- Possibility of data breaches or misuse
- Regulatory and financial consequences of GDPR non-compliance

The project was assessed as **Medium-High Risk** based on the analysis.
